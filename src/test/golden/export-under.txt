# Add a read-through cache

## From the user
> Start with the client.
> Leave billing alone.

## Summary
Put a read-through cache in front of the orders database.

### Why
Reads are slow at peak.

## Sections

### [context] Context
p95 read latency is 900ms at peak.

##### Numbers
- 40k reads/min

### [risks] Risks
Stale reads after a write.
A cold cache after deploys.
